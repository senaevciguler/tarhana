Our Story

Born in Anatolia, crafted in Stockholm. Bringing the ancient wisdom of fermentation to the modern Swedish kitchen.

Modern Tarhana started with a simple realization: the world's oldest instant soup was also one of its healthiest, but it had been forgotten in the rush of modern life.

Our journey began in the sun-drenched plains of Anatolia, where the tradition of fermenting yogurt and vegetables has been passed down through generations. We took this ancient wisdom and brought it to the pristine environment of Sweden.

By combining traditional fermentation techniques with organic Swedish ingredients, we've created a product that is not only rich in history but also perfectly suited for the health-conscious modern consumer.
