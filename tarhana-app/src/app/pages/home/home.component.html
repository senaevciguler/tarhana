What is Tarhana?

Tarhana is a fermented blend of yogurt, whole grains and vegetables, dried and crushed into a nourishing soup base. Just add water, simmer for a few minutes, and enjoy a naturally probiotic, savory meal.

Mildly tangy, savory and comforting. Similar to sourdough depth, with a creamy texture when cooked.
